Land & Utility Surveying

Know your site’s levels, boundaries and buried services before you design, plan or dig.

Discuss your survey ↗ Call 07956 996 424

What you get from a survey

Ryan measures your site as it is today and gives your design team drawings and data they can work from. Knowing the levels, boundaries and buried services early helps avoid redesigns and surprises once work starts.

Topographical & Land Surveying

Measured surveys of the existing ground, site features, boundaries, structures and overhead clearances, done before design or earthworks begin.

  • Detailed topographical & ground level surveysExisting ground levels on greenfield and brownfield sites, with boundaries and physical features mapped.
  • Infrastructure, earthworks & overhead clearancesHighway, bridge and railway surveys, earthworks and stockpile surveys, and overhead cable heights measured for cranes and deliveries.
  • As-built & site boundary surveysSite boundaries, physical features and watercourses mapped, plus as-built records of completed work.
  • Cross-sections, terrain models & CAD drawingsLong sections, cross-sections, digital terrain models (DTMs) and 2D and 3D CAD drawings ready for your design team.

Drainage & Utility Surveying

Finding and mapping existing pipes and cables before you dig, and setting out new drainage to the right line and level.

  • Utility tracing & ground-penetrating radarBuried services detected and traced, utility records coordinated and checked, and ground-penetrating radar (GPR) surveys to locate services before digging.
  • Drainage setting out & pipe invertsNew drainage set out on site, with pipe invert (the inside bottom of the pipe) and gradient surveys on new and existing runs.
  • Manholes, culverts & CCTV checksManhole and chamber surveys, culvert inspections, sonde tracing, pipe locating and CCTV camera surveys of drain condition.
  • Drainage records & network mappingDrainage as-built surveys, network mapping, drainage records and manhole cards.
